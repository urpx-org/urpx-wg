### 3. Approval to Publish

- At the 2026-07-14 session the sense of the room was that publication does not require a formal ballot: the LF Energy technical review is complete and v0.3.0 is a reasonable starting point for the public standard.
- This item records the working group's approval to publish v0.3.0 and to proceed with the license conversion and IP scan. The decision is recorded in the minutes.

---

### 4. License Conversion & IP Scan

- On approval, the specification files convert to the W3C Document License and the dataset files to CDLA-Permissive; source and tooling files stay under Apache-2.0.
- The converted materials then go through the LF Energy IP scan.
- Publication follows once the conversion and the scan are both complete.
