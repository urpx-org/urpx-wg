**URPX is now public**

The Utility Rate Plan Exchange (URPX) — an open standard for machine-readable utility rate plans, hosted by LF Energy Standards and Specifications — is now public at version [LAUNCH-VERSION]. URPX models rate plans as durable identities together with the tariff books, statements, and price feeds that publish their prices — validated end to end with SHACL. The release includes worked test cases built from published Consolidated Edison and Pacific Gas & Electric tariffs, a full data dictionary, and an interactive ontology explorer. The standard will include an API and has plans to incorporate emissions parameters alongside pricing data to support benchmarking and measurement and verification (both under development). Read more about the standard at [urpx.org](https://urpx.org), explore the source at [github.com/urpx-org/urpx](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx), and join the working group at [lists.lfenergy.org/g/urpx-committee](https://lists.lfenergy.org/g/urpx-committee) or subscribe to the mailing list at [lists.lfenergy.org/g/urpx-wg](https://lists.lfenergy.org/g/urpx-wg).
