**URPX is now public**

The Utility Rate Plan Exchange (URPX) — an open standard for machine-readable utility rate plans, hosted by LF Energy Standards and Specifications — is now public at version [LAUNCH-VERSION]. URPX models rate plans as durable identities, the tariff books, statements, and price feeds that publish their prices, and self-contained snapshots with explicit coverage claims — validated end to end with SHACL. The release includes worked test cases built from published Consolidated Edison and Pacific Gas & Electric tariffs, a full data dictionary, and an interactive ontology explorer. Read the standard at [urpx.org](https://urpx.org), explore the source at [github.com/urpx-org/urpx](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx), and join the working group at [lists.lfenergy.org/g/urpx-wg](https://lists.lfenergy.org/g/urpx-wg).
